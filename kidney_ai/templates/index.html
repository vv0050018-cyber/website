Pathology Spectrum

Four Distinct Renal Classification Classes

Classified strictly via statistical intensity moments, GLCM texture, and attenuation densities.

NORMAL

Healthy Renal Parenchyma

Uniform tissue attenuation, preserved corticomedullary differentiation, absence of calculus shadowing or hyperdense masses.

GLCM Contrast: ~1.85 | Attenuation: Balanced
CYST

Renal Simple / Complex Cyst

Well-circumscribed fluid lesion characterized by hypodense attenuation (<20 HU), thin walls, and high internal homogeneity.

Hypodense Ratio: High | Homogeneity: >0.80
TUMOR

Renal Cell Carcinoma (RCC) / Mass

Solid neoplasm exhibiting severe spatial heterogeneity, necrotic irregular margins, microvascular distortion, and elevated GLCM entropy.

GLCM Contrast: >3.5 | Irregular Hu Moments
STONE

Nephrolithiasis / Renal Calculi

Punctate hyperdense calcium concretions (>200 HU) producing marked acoustic shadowing or extreme pixel intensity spikes.

Hyperdense Ratio: Distinct | Kurtosis: >3.5
