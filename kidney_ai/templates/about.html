About the KIDNEY AI Project

Final-Year B.Sc. Computer Science (Artificial Intelligence & Data Science) Capstone Project

Project Aim & Motivation

Kidney diseases—including renal cell carcinoma (tumors), fluid-filled cysts, and nephrolithiasis (kidney stones)—represent critical diagnostic challenges in diagnostic radiology. Early and precise classification of computed tomography (CT) and magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) scans can substantially optimize clinical decision-making.

The primary objective of KIDNEY AI is to construct an authentic, auditable computer-aided diagnosis (CAD) system utilizing classical machine learning algorithms (Support Vector Machine and Decision Tree). Unlike opaque "black-box" deep networks, this system extracts explicit radiomic features (texture, moments, and attenuation values), offering clinical interpretability and dual-model concordance verification.

Key Engineering Principles

  • No Simulated or Fake AI: All predictions originate from trained scikit-learn models serialized via joblib.
  • No Filename Cheating: Image content undergoes strict array decoding, CLAHE filtering, and feature extraction.
  • Dual Algorithm Concordance: The system checks whether SVM and Decision Tree agree, explicitly signaling uncertainty if disagreement occurs.
  • Clinical Privacy & Audit Trail: SQLite database archives all analyses with SHA-256 hashed passwords and audit logging.

Ethical & Medical Disclaimer

KIDNEY AI is strictly an academic, research, and educational demonstration tool. It does not provide medical advice, prescriptions, or definitive diagnostic determinations. All medical decisions must be reviewed by qualified, licensed healthcare providers.
